## In one paragraph

Use foldrun for anything except selling it, or offering it to others as a
product or service that competes with foldrun. Personal use and use inside
your own company are free, at any size. Every version becomes Apache 2.0,
with no restrictions, two years after it is released.

## What you may do, free

- **Use it yourself**: on your laptop, on your own server, for your own
  agents and flows.
- **Use it inside your company or organisation**, of any size, for your own
  work: your team's agents, your internal automations, your CI.
- **Read, change and fork the code**, and keep your changes private or
  share them, as long as the result is used for one of the purposes on
  this list.
- **Teach and research with it** (non-commercial education and research).
- **Set it up for a client as a consultant or agency**, as long as the
  client is the one using it, for their own purposes, under these same
  terms.

## What needs a commercial licence

The licence calls these a *Competing Use*: making foldrun available **to
others** in a **commercial** product or service that substitutes for
foldrun, substitutes for a foldrun product or service that existed when that
version was released, or offers the same or substantially similar
functionality. For example:

- running foldrun, or a modified copy, as a hosted agent platform that other
  people or companies sign up to or pay for;
- reselling it, white-labelling it, or bundling it into a product you sell
  whose value is what foldrun does;
- selling access to a managed foldrun instance.

If you want to do any of these, or are not sure whether your use counts,
write to **hello@foldrun.io**. Commercial licences are available.

## The two-year rule

Each version is offered under FSL from the day it is released. On the
second anniversary of that release, that version is **also** available under
the Apache License 2.0 — irrevocably, for everyone, for any purpose. Newer
versions are not affected until they reach their own second anniversary.
